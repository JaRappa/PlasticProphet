Privacy Policy

Plastic Prophet is built with privacy in mind. This page describes what data is collected and how it is used.

What we collect

  • App diagnostics and analytics (opt-in).
  • Precise location data (GPS coordinates) when you grant location permission.
  • Emails (and names) you give us to join our waitlist — used only to notify you about launch and related updates.
  • Basic account info (email and name) when you create an account.
  • Optional diagnostics and location data only if you enable them in the app.

How we use data

We use collected data to improve recommendations, surface relevant offers, and make the app more reliable. Precise location data helps determine the types of places you visit so we can suggest which credit card rewards are most relevant at those locations. We will use emails provided in the waitlist form for launch notifications and account emails for sign-in, account messages, and to personalize your experience.

Account and financial data

Plastic Prophet requires an account to store preferences and non-sensitive metadata. We never collect or store full card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account numbers.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the waitlist email by contacting us.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your account data — we will comply unless legally required to retain it.
  • Turn off location or analytics in your device/app settings to stop those collections.

Security & third parties

Accounts and authentication are provided by Amazon Cognito. We do not sell personal data. We may use trusted service providers to operate the service and store data on secure servers.

Contact

Email Help@PlasticProphet.com with questions or data requests.
