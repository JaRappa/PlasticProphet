🚀 Getting Started: Create an Account

Plastic Prophet uses secure OAuth 2.0 authentication powered by AWS Cognito to keep your account safe. You'll never need to remember another password!

  1. Download the App
    Get Plastic Prophet from the iOS App Store (requires iOS 17 or later).
  2. Open the App
    Launch Plastic Prophet. You'll see the welcome screen with the Plastic Prophet logo.
  3. Tap "Sign In" or "Get Started"
    The app will open a secure browser window (Safari) for authentication.
  4. Create Your Account
    In the Cognito Hosted UI, tap "Sign Up" and enter:
    • Your email address
    • Choose a secure password (min. 8 characters)
    • Optional: First and last name
  5. Verify Your Email
    Check your inbox for a verification code and enter it when prompted.
  6. Complete Sign In
    After verification, sign in with your email and password. The app will automatically authenticate you.
Privacy First: Your authentication is handled entirely by AWS Cognito. Plastic Prophet never sees or stores your password. All communication is encrypted end-to-end.

After your first sign-in, you'll go through a quick onboarding process to set up your account.

Welcome Screen

You'll be greeted with a welcome message and the Plastic Prophet logo. Tap "Let's Get Started!" to continue.

Terms of Service

  1. Read the Terms

Permissions Setup

Next, you'll be asked to grant permissions for key app features (see the iOS Permissions section for details).

Add Your First Cards

The onboarding flow will guide you to add your credit cards. You can add them now or skip and add them later from the Wallet view.

💳 Adding Your Cards

Plastic Prophet makes it easy to add your credit cards. You have three options:

🔍 Search

Browse and search from a catalog of popular credit cards. Perfect if you know the card name.

📷 Camera Scan

Scan your physical card with your camera to quickly capture card details (requires camera permission).

✍️ Manual Entry

Manually enter card details if your card isn't in the catalog or you prefer not to scan.

Method 1: Search for Your Card

  1. Open the Wallet
    From the main app screen, tap the "Wallet" tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap the + Button
    In the bottom-right corner, tap the green floating action button (FAB) with the + icon.
  3. Select "Search Cards"
    From the menu, tap "Search Cards".
  4. Search and Select
    Use the search bar to find your card by name or issuer. Tap on the card tile to select it.
  5. Tap "Done"
    Your selected card(s) will be added to your wallet.

Method 2: Scan with Camera

  1. Open Card Search
    Follow steps 1-3 above to open the card search view.
  2. Tap the Camera Icon
    Next to the search bar, tap the camera icon.
  3. Grant Camera Permission
    If prompted, allow Plastic Prophet to access your camera (see iOS Permissions).
  4. Scan Your Card
    Position your credit card in the camera frame. The app will automatically detect card details.
  5. Confirm Details
    Review the captured information and confirm to add the card.

Method 3: Manual Entry

  1. Open the FAB Menu
    From the Wallet view, tap the + button.
  2. Select "Enter Manually"
    Tap "Enter Manually" from the menu.
  3. Fill in Card Details
    Enter the following information:
    • Card name or nickname
    • Card network (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
    • Last 4 digits (for identification only)
    • Reward summary (optional, e.g., "3% on dining")
  4. Save the Card
    Tap "Save" to add the card to your wallet.
Privacy & Security: Plastic Prophet does not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or any sensitive financial data. We only store card names, networks, and reward structures to provide recommendations. You're always in control of your data.

🗑️ Removing a Card

If you no longer use a card or want to update your wallet, you can easily remove cards.

  1. Open Your Wallet
    Navigate to the Wallet tab from the main navigation.
  2. Find the Card
    Scroll through your card list and locate the card you want to remove.
  3. Swipe to Delete
    Swipe left on the card entry to reveal the delete button.
  4. Confirm Removal
    Tap "Delete" or the trash icon. You may be asked to confirm this action.
Note: Removing a card from Plastic Prophet only removes it from your app profile—it does not affect your actual credit card account or close the card with your issuer.

🔐 Setting Up iOS Permissions

Plastic Prophet requires certain iOS permissions to provide the best experience. Here's what we need and why:

📷 Camera Access

Why: To scan your credit cards for quick setup.

Required: Only if you want to use card scanning feature.

📍 Location Access

Why: To detect nearby merchants and provide location-based card recommendations.

Required: Yes, for core recommendation features.

Granting Permissions During Onboarding

  1. Permission Request Screen
    During onboarding, you'll see the Permissions screen explaining what's needed.
  2. Grant Camera Permission
    Tap "Grant Camera Access". iOS will show a system popup—tap "Allow" or "OK".
  3. Grant Location Permission
    Tap "Grant Location Access". iOS will show a system popup—tap "Allow While Using App" (recommended).
  4. Continue Setup
    After granting permissions, tap "Continue" to proceed.

Changing Permissions Later

If you skip permissions during setup or want to change them later:

  1. Open iOS Settings
    Exit Plastic Prophet and open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll to Plastic Prophet
    Scroll down in Settings until you find "Plastic Prophet" in the app list.
  3. Tap Plastic Prophet
    You'll see a settings page specific to the app.
  4. Toggle Permissions
    You'll see switches for:
    • Location: Choose "Never", "Ask Next Time", "While Using the App", or "Always"
    • Camera: Toggle on or off
  5. Return to App
    Go back to Plastic Prophet. Your permission changes will take effect immediately.

Checking Permission Status in App

You can verify your current permissions within Plastic Prophet:

  • Navigate to Settings from the main tab bar
  • Look at the Permissions section
  • You'll see green checkmarks for granted permissions and red X marks for denied permissions
  • Tap "Open Settings" if available to quickly jump to iOS Settings
Privacy: Plastic Prophet only uses location data to identify nearby merchants for recommendations. We never track your location continuously or store your location history. Camera access is only used when you actively scan a card.

🎯 Using Card Recommendations

The core feature of Plastic Prophet is smart card recommendations based on your location and spending patterns.

How Recommendations Work

Plastic Prophet analyzes:

  • Your current location
  • Nearby merchant categories (e.g., restaurant, gas station, grocery store)
  • Your credit card rewards structures
  • Historical spending patterns (future feature)

Getting a Recommendation

  1. Ensure Location is Enabled
    Make sure you've granted location permission (see iOS Permissions).
  2. Open the App
    When you're near a merchant or store, open Plastic Prophet.
  3. View Recommendation
    The main screen will show the best card to use at your current location, along with the reward you'll earn.
  4. Use Your Card
    Follow the recommendation to maximize your rewards!

Understanding Recommendations

Each recommendation shows:

  • Card Name: Which card to use
  • Reward Rate: How many points or cash back you'll earn
  • Category: The merchant category detected (e.g., "Dining", "Gas", "Grocery")
  • Why This Card: Brief explanation of the recommendation
Coming Soon: Advanced features like spending analytics, monthly reward summaries, and personalized optimization tips are in development.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

I Can't Sign In

  • Verify your email address has been confirmed (check your inbox for verification email)
  • Make sure you're using the correct email and password
  • Try the "Forgot Password" flow to reset your password
  • Check your internet connection—authentication requires an active connection

Camera Scanning Isn't Working

  • Verify camera permission is granted (see iOS Permissions)
  • Make sure your card is well-lit and in focus
  • Try manual entry if scanning continues to fail

No Recommendations Showing

  • Ensure location permission is granted (app needs "While Using App" access minimum)
  • Make sure you've added at least one card to your wallet
  • Try moving to a different location or waiting a few moments for location to update
  • Check your internet connection—recommendations require API access

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Make sure you're running the latest version of the app from the App Store
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Try reinstalling the app (your cloud data will be preserved)

My Cards Aren't Syncing

  • Check your internet connection
  • Sign out and sign back in to refresh your data
  • Your cards are stored in your account profile, not locally
