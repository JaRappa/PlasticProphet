Plastic Prophet

Smarter credit card picks, automatically

Plastic Prophet makes sure you’re always spending wisely and staying in the profit — we help you find the best credit card to maximize points and savings based on where you are, whether that's a coffee shop, a grocery store, or the gas station down the street.

How it works

• Requires an account to personalize recommendations.
• Must be online — card suggestions rely on updated reward data.
• iOS only (no Android planned).

Data & Privacy

Plastic Prophet uses spending categories, past choices, and location context to improve card suggestions. Usage analytics are collected to refine recommendation models. We do not sell personal data.
